About Us & Our Expertise

The FON NGWASHI LAW OFFICES is a full service law firm with the know-how to handle the legal needs of clients in Cameroon's peculiar business environment. With offices in the cosmopolitan city of Douala, we are strategically placed to offer top quality legal services to our clients in the major economic hubs of the country and of the CEMAC and OHADA spaces.

The Firm has distinguished itself in delivering bespoke services to satisfy its clients over the years. With a broad outlook, making us one of the few truly full-service law firms in Cameroon, we have developed mastery over a wide range of legal matters that border the litigious and the transactional, covering Corporate & Commercial, Energy & Natural Resources, Telecommunications, Foreign Investment, Banking & Finance, Shipping & Maritime, Aviation, Taxation, Commercial Litigation, Immigration, Alternative Dispute Resolution and Project Finance.
