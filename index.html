About Us & Our Expertise / À Propos & Expertise

The FON NGWASHI LAW OFFICES is a full service law firm with the know-how to handle the legal needs of clients in Cameroon's peculiar business environment. With offices in the cosmopolitan city of Douala, we are strategically placed to offer top quality legal services to our clients in the major economic hubs of the country and of the CEMAC and OHADA spaces.

Le cabinet FON NGWASHI LAW OFFICES est un cabinet d'avocats pluridisciplinaire doté du savoir-faire nécessaire pour répondre aux besoins juridiques des clients dans l'environnement commercial particulier du Cameroun. Basés à Douala, nous offrons des services de qualité dans les espaces CEMAC et OHADA.

The Firm has distinguished itself in delivering bespoke services to satisfy its clients over the years, covering Corporate & Commercial, Energy & Natural Resources, Telecommunications, Foreign Investment, Banking & Finance, Shipping & Maritime, Aviation, Taxation, Commercial Litigation, Immigration, Alternative Dispute Resolution and Project Finance.
